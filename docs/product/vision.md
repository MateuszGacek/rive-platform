# Product vision — working draft

**Status:** Discovery. This document captures the owner's product intent, **not** approved scope, architecture or MVP.

## Confirmed intent

- **rivé** is a hospitality and wellness business in Bielsko-Biała, evolving from the existing Cezar business.
- The goal is a real, maintainable operational platform—not a technology demo.
- The owner intends to lead business discovery, approve architecture and quality, and deliver features with the help of coding agents.
- This project should also demonstrate disciplined end-to-end engineering through meaningful decisions, tests and operated releases.
- The platform should eventually connect customer-facing experiences with day-to-day staff and management operations.

## Candidate business capabilities — not committed scope

- Public website and managed marketing content.
- Availability and booking of accommodation, wellness services, packages and events.
- Guest and reception workflows.
- Employee schedules, time records and service assignments.
- Customer charges, deposits, payment status and financial reporting.
- External integrations such as booking channels, payment providers, calendars and accounting systems.

These areas are starting points for discovery, **not** a finalized feature list or modular architecture.

## Candidate users — validate

Guests, reception staff, service providers, management, marketing, event organizers and accounting. Their actual tasks and access rights must be mapped from real workflows.

## Success measures — to agree with the owner

We still need to establish which operational problems are highest priority, which existing systems will remain, how much manual work should be reduced, and which reliability/financial outcomes matter most.

## Decisions explicitly not made

No first-release scope, stack, CMS, application count, API architecture, database model, or build-vs-buy result has been approved. A shared API / monorepo and modular monolith are **ideas to evaluate**, not mandates.
