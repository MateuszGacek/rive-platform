# rivé Platform

**Status:** Product discovery — no MVP, system architecture, or technology stack approved.

This repository is the version-controlled product and engineering workspace for **rivé**, a hospitality and wellness business in Bielsko-Biała. The objective is to understand the business, make traceable decisions, and build reliable software incrementally with AI coding agents under human ownership.

## Working agreement

We plan the **whole product at a high level** and specify **only the next valuable, reviewable slice** in detail. User-provided business input outranks AI suggestions. Proposals are not decisions.

The repository is **public**. Never commit guest or employee records, personal information, access credentials, internal financial figures, or other confidential business data. Use anonymized examples instead.
